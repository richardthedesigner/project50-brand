Contrast rules

  • Text pairs: Pitch on Chalk, Chalk on Pitch, Tape on Pitch.
  • Summer Tape and Court Clay are never text on Chalk.
  • Track Blue reads at body size on Chalk, large sizes on Pitch.

Typography

Syne 700–800

Display, hero statements, used sparingly.

Plus Jakarta Sans 300–800

Body and UI, roughly 90% of all brand communication.

Logo

  • Wordmark minimum 120px digital, 30mm print.
  • Monogram minimum 32px digital, 10mm print.
  • Clear space: the height of the "0", all sides.
  • Approved colourways only. No gradients, shadows, or effects. Clean backgrounds.

Spacing & motion

  • Base unit 8px; scale 8 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 64 / 80 / 120.
  • Radius 4 / 8 / 12px.
  • Motion: 150 / 250 / 400ms. Nothing longer than 400ms. Honour reduced motion completely.

Programme tiers

Foundations · Form · Flow · Field · Fifty

Voice

  • Measured and editorial. Confident without bombast.
  • Mantras: "Care is strength." "Capacity is performance."
  • Never: "shred", "beast mode", "no pain, no gain", countdown timers, transformation photos.
